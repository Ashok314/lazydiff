Help and information

Separate patterns with commas. Plain patterns show matching files; ! hides them.

!*.test.ts, !*.spec.ts hides test and spec files.

The popup and in-page bar share the same filter. Counts reflect files currently loaded by GitHub.

Filters stay in local Chrome storage. No data is sent anywhere.
